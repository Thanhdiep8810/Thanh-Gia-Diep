Our Mission

We are here to make
your words work
for you.

AI is incredibly useful. But most of the time, what it produces sounds like a press release written by a committee. It does not sound like you — and that matters more than people realize.

At Diep Studio, every tool we build solves the same problem: making your words work for you. Not replacing you. Just making the hard parts feel a little less hard.

We believe everyone deserves to communicate with confidence. That is why every tool we build is free to try, simple to use, and built around you.

"
"I built these tools because I kept running into the same problem: AI is everywhere, but most of what it produces sounds hollow. I just wanted something that helped people sound like themselves again."
— Thanh Diep, Founder
What We Stand For

keeping it simple.

The Founder

One person.
Genuine care.

Diep Studio was started by Thanh Diep — someone who got tired of AI writing that sounded nothing like a real person. Every tool here came from a genuine frustration, built to fix something that actually mattered.

The name Diep means deep in Vietnamese. That shows up in everything — the thought behind each tool, the attention to detail, and the care for the people who use them every day.

This is not a big company. It is one person, building tools they wish existed — with care, intention, and a genuine desire to make people's lives a little easier.
