ATS Tips
✦
Use simple formatting. ATS systems struggle with tables, columns, and graphics. Plain text wins.
→
Mirror the job description. Use the exact keywords and phrases from the posting — not synonyms.
%
Quantify everything. "Grew revenue 40%" beats "improved revenue" every time. Numbers get parsed.
H
Standard section headers. Use "Work Experience", "Education", "Skills" — not creative variations.
⊕
Include a skills section. Dedicated keyword sections dramatically improve ATS parsing accuracy.
✓
One page for under 10 years. ATS systems rank shorter, denser resumes higher in most cases.
